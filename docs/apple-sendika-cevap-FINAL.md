## Vatansever-Sen Sağlık (app 6802027116, submission 7be3a4de-2ea0-41b9-b0a9-e2a5587d3bc9)

```
Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the detailed message. We respectfully ask for reconsideration under 4.3(a), 4.3(b) and 4.2.6. A signed and stamped authorization letter from the union's General President (Adem Akyol) is attached.

1. What the app does. Vatansever-Sen Sağlık is the official mobile app of Vatansever Kamu Görevlileri Sağlık Sendikası (VATANSEVER-SEN-SAĞLIK), a legally registered labor union in Türkiye whose statute is published by the Ministry of Labour and Social Security (https://www.csgb.gov.tr/Media/3gync051/vatansever-sen-sa%C4%9Flik.pdf). Eligible public health employees apply for membership online; members then use the digital membership card with QR verification, union announcements and notifications, documents, surveys, legal-support and member-request channels, and in-app account deletion. It replaces paper forms and phone calls between the union and its members.

2. Intended user. Public-sector health and social-service employees in Türkiye who are, or want to become, members of this union (the union's area of operation is nationwide).

3. Gap. The member services, member records, documents and communications of this one union exist nowhere else on the App Store. Its members have no other app; this is not a generic app in an existing category.

4. Beta testing. The developer tested the app personally on physical devices through TestFlight. When App Review reported a launch failure on iPad Air, we fixed it in build 2 (bundled local startup view, timeout and recovery handling, iPad device-family support).

5. Standalone or suite? Each union app serves a separate legal organisation and its own membership community; they are not variants for one audience. They share a common technology platform (interface and security infrastructure) maintained by us as technology provider. Our other published apps (an exam-preparation app, a public-employee guide) are unrelated.

6. Could it be consolidated into another app? No. A union's members expect their own organisation's app, with its own name, brand, member database, privacy controller and notifications. Merging organisations would mix member data and identities, which neither the unions nor their members want.

7. Shared code/assets. Common interface and infrastructure components are shared for accessibility, security and maintenance. Each app has its own bundle ID, branding, content, backend, member database, documents, notification channel and privacy policy.

8. Third-party SDKs. Only standard components (for example OneSignal for push notifications). No third-party content library.

9. Created for a client? Yes. We develop and maintain the app as technology provider for the union, which owns the brand and is the content provider. The union has authorised us in writing to submit and manage the app (signed and stamped letter attached). If Apple requires the union to be the account holder under 4.2.6, please tell us and we will coordinate with the union to submit it under its own account.

We can provide a valid review account, screenshots and a screen recording of the organisation-specific functions. If a specific binary, metadata or function looks duplicative, please tell us which one.

Thank you,
Oğuzhan Terzioğlu — Ayas Yazılım
```
